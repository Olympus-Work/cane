- Decides at every settled funding event. Entry: avg funding APR over the last N ≥ entry APR, last rate > 0, break-even ≤ max days (B4, B5). Exit: avg < exit APR or the last K rates negative (B14).
- Both legs fill at the 1h open of the settlement hour; the entry settlement is not earned, the exit settlement is. Same quantity on both legs, no step rounding.
- Fees per fill: spot 0.075%, perp 0.045%; slippage allowance 0.050% per round trip, half at each side (B6).
- Funding on the short = qty × mark × rate (mark from the funding row, else the perp 1h open).
- Capital used = notional + margin at 2x + BNB buffer for 10 round trips (B23, B26), counted for the whole window. Net APR = net / capital used × 365 / days.
- Margin stress: worst perp 1h high while open, isolated, maintenance rate 0.50%, funding not counted, no rebalance simulated. 100% = margin gone.
- A pair open at the window end is marked to market at the last 1h close, with exit costs.
- This is evidence for choosing thresholds (spec AC2), not a forecast.
